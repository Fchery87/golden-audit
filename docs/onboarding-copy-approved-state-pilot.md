# Onboarding copy: approved-state pilot gating

> **Status:** Draft UX copy for the current recommended one-state free pilot posture. This is product copy, not legal advice.

## Eligibility step title
**Pilot availability**

## Primary message
This pilot is currently available only to consumers in **approved pilot states**.

At this time, the approved pilot state is:
- **California**

## Supporting copy
This product is a **free, educational credit-report analysis pilot**.
It is designed to help you understand what your uploaded credit report shows and highlight items you may want to review further.

It does **not**:
- repair your credit,
- send disputes for you,
- contact credit bureaus or furnishers on your behalf,
- guarantee score improvement,
- or provide legal conclusions.

## State confirmation prompt
**Please confirm your state of residence.**

### Option label
- `I currently reside in California`
- `I do not currently reside in California`

## Continue-state helper text
You can continue only if you currently reside in an approved pilot state.

## Blocked-state message
This pilot is not currently available in your state.

We are limiting access while we review launch scope state by state.
This does **not** mean anything is wrong with your report or that you are ineligible for future access.

## Blocked-state follow-up text
You may check back later as pilot availability expands.

## Authorization transition copy
Before uploading a report, you will be asked to confirm that:
- the report is yours or you are authorized to use it,
- you are using this service for your **personal educational review**,
- and you understand how your report data will be used, retained, and deleted during this free pilot.

## Tone guardrails
This screen should avoid:
- any promise of credit repair,
- any implication that broader U.S. availability already exists,
- any suggestion that the product will act on the consumer's behalf,
- any claim of score improvement or guaranteed results.

## Short version for compact UI
**California-only pilot**
This free pilot is currently available only to California consumers. The service provides educational credit-report analysis only — not credit repair, disputes, score guarantees, or legal conclusions.
