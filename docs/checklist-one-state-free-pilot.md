# Checklist: one-state free pilot

> **Status:** Execution checklist for the currently recommended rollout posture. This is not legal advice.

## Goal
Launch only a **free, invite-only, analysis-only pilot** in **one reviewed state** while preserving the current product boundary and avoiding any nationwide or paid-launch overclaim.

## 1. Scope decision
- [ ] Name the single reviewed launch state.
- [ ] Record why this state was chosen.
- [ ] Confirm no marketing or onboarding copy implies broader availability.
- [ ] Confirm unsupported states are excluded from the pilot scope.

## 2. Product boundary
- [ ] Keep the product **analysis-only** and **educational**.
- [ ] Keep the product **consumer-uploaded** and **consumer-only**.
- [ ] Keep disputes, bureau contact, furnisher contact, and legal conclusions out of scope.
- [ ] Keep payment, subscriptions, or paid upsells out of scope for the pilot.

## 3. Copy and positioning
- [ ] Apply `docs/copy-boundary-guide.md` to onboarding, UI, and marketing copy.
- [ ] Remove any repair / fix / improve-score / deletion / bureau-intervention language.
- [ ] Ensure testimonials, if ever added, emphasize understanding and clarity rather than removals or score gains.
- [ ] Add clear language that the pilot is available only in the approved launch state.

## 4. Eligibility / access gating
- [ ] Add a launch-scope field such as `launchState` or `approvedStates` to the governance model.
- [ ] Require explicit state-of-residence confirmation before upload.
- [ ] Prevent unsupported states from proceeding into the pilot flow.
- [ ] Ensure support workflows do not bypass state gating casually.

## 5. Legal / privacy / security alignment
- [ ] Keep the written authorization current and versioned.
- [ ] Keep privacy notice / retention disclosures aligned with the one-state pilot scope.
- [ ] Maintain the current no-sale / no-ad / no-unrelated-training posture.
- [ ] Maintain GLBA-style safeguards posture until formally cleared otherwise.

## 6. Pilot-only business posture
- [ ] Keep the pilot genuinely free.
- [ ] Avoid paid plan language, upgrade prompts, or recurring-service framing.
- [ ] Avoid broad U.S.-availability language in FAQ / support / landing pages.
- [ ] Avoid implying that the pilot is a repair service or dispute service.

## 7. Required human approvals
- [ ] Product approval recorded.
- [ ] Legal approval recorded.
- [ ] Privacy approval recorded.
- [ ] Security approval recorded.
- [ ] Operations approval recorded.
- [ ] Accessibility approval recorded.
- [ ] Vendor approval recorded.

## 8. Evidence to record
- [ ] Evidence reference for the chosen state and rationale.
- [ ] Evidence reference for the launch-scope copy review.
- [ ] Evidence reference for the state-gating behavior.
- [ ] Evidence reference for pilot-free pricing posture.
- [ ] Evidence reference for the final approval record entries.

## 9. Do not do during this posture
- [ ] Do not claim nationwide availability.
- [ ] Do not add paid pricing.
- [ ] Do not add dispute-generation features.
- [ ] Do not add lender / landlord / employer sharing flows.
- [ ] Do not add copy that resembles credit repair brands.

## Completion condition
This checklist is complete only when:
- one reviewed state is explicitly chosen,
- the product is visibly limited to that scope,
- the pilot remains free and analysis-only,
- and the required human approvals are actually recorded.
