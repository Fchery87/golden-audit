# Checklist: pause launch claims until counsel review

> **Status:** Execution checklist for the most conservative no-launch-claims posture. This is not legal advice.

## Goal
Avoid any live launch-scope claim until formal legal review occurs, while continuing internal product-shaping and safety work.

## 1. Public posture
- [ ] Avoid claiming live availability in any state.
- [ ] Avoid implying a current public pilot.
- [ ] Avoid broad U.S. availability or waitlist copy that overstates readiness.

## 2. Product boundary work
- [ ] Keep refining the analysis-only educational boundary.
- [ ] Keep copy aligned with `docs/copy-boundary-guide.md`.
- [ ] Keep payment, repair, dispute, and sharing features out of scope.

## 3. Internal readiness work
- [ ] Continue improving authorization, privacy, security, and deletion artifacts.
- [ ] Continue refining state-by-state research notes.
- [ ] Continue improving product/spec clarity without making launch claims.

## 4. Trigger to exit this posture
- [ ] A qualified review path is identified (e.g. counsel or equivalent formal review).
- [ ] A narrower launch posture is chosen and documented.
- [ ] Launch copy is reviewed against the current boundary docs.

## Completion condition
This checklist is complete only when the team intentionally moves to a narrower active launch posture or obtains enough formal review to replace the pause.
