# State mini memo: Utah

> **Status:** Preliminary state memo from regulator materials. Not legal advice.

## Question
Does Utah have an explicit regime for credit service organizations that should make us cautious about any paid or repair-adjacent product positioning?

## What surfaced
Utah's Division of Consumer Protection has a direct **Credit Service Organizations** page.

Captured regulator language states that a credit services organization and related salespersons/agents/representatives may not conduct business regulated by the Credit Services Organizations Act in Utah without first securing:
- a **certificate of registration**, and
- the **required surety**.

## What this means for this product
### Verified
- Utah has an explicit Credit Service Organizations regulatory structure.
- Registration/surety requirements exist for business falling within the Act.

### Best current inference
Utah is not a state where we should rely on broad assumptions. If the product is paid and/or positioned as improving credit or offering credit-help services beyond education/analysis, Utah risk increases materially.

## Practical conclusion
- Utah is a **priority review state**.
- Keep the product boundary tightly analysis-only and educational.
- Do not assume Utah is cleared for paid launch without more specific review.
