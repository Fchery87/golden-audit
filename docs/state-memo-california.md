# State mini memo: California

> **Status:** Preliminary state memo from regulator materials. Not legal advice.

## Question
Does current California regulator material suggest that a consumer-facing, analysis-only credit-report platform is clearly outside California regulation, or does California remain a high-risk state requiring careful scope control?

## What surfaced
California DFPI materials show active oversight of adjacent consumer-financial assistance categories under the **California Consumer Financial Protection Law (CCFPL)**.

Direct signal captured:
- DFPI states the CCFPL expanded the Department's authority to oversee financial products and services previously unregulated by the Department.
- DFPI separately states that effective Feb. 15, 2025, no person may provide **debt settlement services** to California residents without first registering with DFPI.

## What this means for this product
### Verified
- California has an active regulator and expanded oversight posture in adjacent categories.
- Debt settlement services are explicitly regulated.
- California is **not** a state where we should assume a novel credit-adjacent product is automatically outside regulation.

### Best current inference
An analysis-only platform is **not the same thing** as debt settlement services, but California remains a high-scrutiny state. The product should keep its boundary narrow and avoid any repair, negotiation, settlement, or bureau-contact positioning.

## Practical conclusion
- California remains a **priority review state**.
- Do not treat California as automatically cleared for paid launch.
- Keep the current analysis-only / educational / consumer-only boundary if California remains in scope.
