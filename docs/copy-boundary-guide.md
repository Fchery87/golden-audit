# Allowed / banned copy guide for the analysis-only product boundary

> **Status:** Draft writing guide for product, UX, support, and marketing surfaces. This guide is intended to preserve the analysis-only educational posture reflected in the current legal and positioning research. It is not legal advice.

## Purpose

This product should read like an **educational credit-report analysis tool**, not like a **credit repair** or **dispute execution** service.

Use this guide when writing:
- landing pages
- onboarding copy
- upload/authorization screens
- analysis report copy
- CTA labels
- FAQs
- support macros
- email copy
- ads
- testimonials

## Positioning sentence

Preferred core description:

> A consumer-facing educational platform that analyzes a credit report the consumer uploads, explains what the report shows, highlights inconsistencies for the consumer to review, and returns findings only to that consumer.

## Allowed language

### Product-level framing
Prefer:
- credit report analysis
- credit report review
- educational report review
- understand your credit report
- explain what your report shows
- highlight bureau differences
- identify inconsistencies for your review
- verification recommended
- evidence-linked findings
- educational opportunities
- consumer-controlled next steps
- report literacy
- understand what may need follow-up
- understand your rights
- understand reported balances, dates, statuses, and inquiries

### CTA examples
Prefer:
- Review my report
- Analyze my report
- Understand my report
- See my findings
- Compare bureau entries
- Review possible inconsistencies
- Learn what this item means

### Output/report language
Prefer:
- observed fact
- inconsistency
- verification recommended
- insufficient information
- educational opportunity
- possible alternative explanation
- based on the uploaded report
- may warrant verification
- consider reviewing with the reporting source
- consider consulting qualified counsel / a nonprofit counselor where appropriate

## Banned or highly discouraged language

### Repair / improvement language
Do not use:
- credit repair
- fix your credit
- clean up your credit
- remove negative items
- delete items from your report
- erase negatives
- boost your score
- improve your score
- get approved faster
- raise your score
- restore your credit

### Dispute / intervention language
Do not use:
- we dispute
- we will dispute
- send disputes for you
- challenge bureaus on your behalf
- contact the bureau
- contact the furnisher
- creditor interventions
- debt validation letters
- cease and desist letters
- goodwill letters
- legal interventions

### Legal-conclusion language
Do not use:
- this violates the FCRA
- illegal reporting
- you have a legal claim
- legally invalid debt
- legally unenforceable
- entitled to deletion
- entitled to damages
- sue the bureau
- file a lawsuit

### Outcome-promise language
Do not use:
- guaranteed results
- guaranteed deletions
- guaranteed score increase
- fastest way to improve your credit
- proven removals
- better approval odds
- pay us to improve your credit

## Gray-area language to use carefully

These phrases are not automatically banned, but they should be reviewed carefully and usually reframed:
- improve your credit profile
- credit help
- fix errors
- take action
- recommended solution
- next best move
- optimize
- audit

Preferred reframes:
- improve your understanding of your report
- identify information you may want to verify
- review questions to investigate
- educational next steps
- structured report analysis

## Testimonials guidance

Avoid testimonials emphasizing:
- removals
- score jumps
- approvals
- repaired credit
- disputes won
- legal pressure

Prefer testimonials emphasizing:
- clarity
- understanding
- organization
- confidence in reviewing the report
- easier comparison across bureaus
- feeling more informed about next steps

## UX and onboarding implications

### Should say
- You are uploading a report you already have and are authorized to use.
- This service is for your personal educational review.
- Findings are based on the displayed report and may require verification.
- You remain in control of any next steps.

### Should not say
- We will help repair your credit.
- We will challenge negative items.
- We will improve your score.
- We act on your behalf with bureaus or furnishers.

## Escalation rule

If copy does any of the following, route it for additional review before use:
- implies score improvement,
- implies removal or deletion,
- implies disputes or bureau contact,
- implies legal entitlement or violation,
- implies guaranteed consumer outcomes,
- resembles recurring repair-service marketing.
