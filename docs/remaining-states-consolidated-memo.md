> **Status:** Preliminary research memo from the final grouped Exa-backed state sweep. Not legal advice.

## Purpose
This memo closes the current first-pass nationwide scan by summarizing the states that were not covered by the earlier priority/follow-up mini memos.

## Most important new signal from this pass

### West Virginia — explicit regime surfaced
West Virginia surfaced a direct state page for **Credit Service Organizations** indicating that certain companies must register with the Secretary of State as a Credit Service Organization under state code.

**Implication:** West Virginia should be upgraded from unresolved to **explicit-regime surfaced / priority review**.

Source surfaced:
- `https://sos.wv.gov/licensing/other-registrations/credit-service-organizations`

## What this final sweep supports

### Verified takeaway
- The nationwide map is still **not** a blanket yes/no answer.
- Some states clearly surface explicit or adjacent regimes.
- Many states remain unresolved after first-pass search.
- That means the honest posture is still **state-by-state rollout**, not nationwide clearance.

### Strongest practical implication
The product should continue to maintain the safest boundary already established in the repo:
- analysis-only,
- educational,
- consumer-uploaded,
- consumer-only delivery,
- no disputes,
- no bureau/furnisher contact,
- no score-improvement or repair promises,
- no paid nationwide claim.
